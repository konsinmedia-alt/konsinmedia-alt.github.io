Privacy Policy

Konsin Media Content Manager — by Konsin Media, operated by Sigma Media.
Effective date: 10 October 2026. Last updated: 10 October 2026.

1. Overview

Konsin Media Content Manager is a private, internal tool used exclusively by Konsin Media and its authorized staff to manage the company's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API. The app is not offered to external users, and we do not operate a public service that collects data from the general public.

2. Data we access

With the user's consent via Google OAuth, the app requests the following scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — read channel and video information (e.g., video IDs, titles, statuses) needed to manage our own channels;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload video files to channels we own;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl — view, edit, and manage video metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy status, scheduling) for channels we own.

The app only accesses data for YouTube channels owned or managed by Konsin Media / Sigma Media. It does not request access to, and does not process, data belonging to unrelated third parties.

3. How we use the data

Data accessed through the YouTube Data API is used solely for the following internal purposes:

  • uploading video files produced by our team;
  • reading channel/video information to verify uploads and channel state;
  • editing video metadata and publish scheduling for our own channels.

No other use is made of the data.

4. Limited use — YouTube API Services compliance

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the YouTube API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • we do not sell, rent, or otherwise transfer user data to third parties;
  • we do not use the data for advertising purposes or for serving ads;
  • we do not allow humans to read the data except where necessary for the purposes described above (e.g., troubleshooting by an authorized administrator);
  • we do not combine or aggregate the data with other datasets for profiling or analytics beyond the internal management functions described above.

5. Data storage and security

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in access-restricted files (operating-system file permissions) on access-controlled infrastructure accessible only to authorized administrators of Konsin Media / Sigma Media. Channel metadata processed by the app is stored only for as long as needed for internal operations and is protected by standard access controls. Tokens are revoked and deleted when no longer needed.

6. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose data obtained through the YouTube Data API to any third party, except as required by law or to comply with a valid legal process.

7. Your rights

Because the app is internal and its only users are Konsin Media / Sigma Media staff:

  • any authorized user may request a copy, correction, or deletion of data relating to their use of the app;
  • any user may revoke the app's access at any time via Google account permissions.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The current version will always be published at this URL.
