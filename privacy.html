Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

YouTube Content OS is a private application used to manage authorized YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

Information Accessed

The application accesses authorized YouTube channel information and permissions necessary to upload and schedule videos.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to manage channels, upload videos, and schedule publications.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored securely in controlled environments and are not sold to third parties.

Data Sharing

The application communicates with Google and YouTube APIs to perform authorized actions.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke access through Google Account security settings at https://myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
